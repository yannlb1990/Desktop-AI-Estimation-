# Metricore — Product Demo Video Script
_Duration: 75 seconds | Format: Screen recording + voiceover + captions_

---

## Production Notes

- **Tone**: Calm, confident, Australian accent preferred. Not salesy. Like a colleague showing you the tool.
- **Pace**: Unhurried. Let the product breathe — don't rush through screens.
- **Music**: Minimal background track, low volume. Subtle, modern, neutral. Fade under voiceover.
- **Resolution**: Record at 1440p or higher. Crop to 16:9.
- **Cursor**: Use a large, visible cursor. Move deliberately — no fast darting.
- **Plan to use**: A residential PDF plan — 3–4 rooms, clear labels. Looks real, not a toy example.

---

## Full Script with Screen Direction

---

### [0:00 – 0:07] — OPENING

**Screen:**
Blank Metricore dashboard. Clean. No projects yet.
Cursor moves to "New Project" button.

**Voiceover:**
> "Most builders spend hours manually measuring plans and pricing up jobs before they've won a single contract."

**Caption (bottom third):**
`Most builders spend hours just to price one job.`

---

### [0:07 – 0:14] — UPLOAD

**Screen:**
New project screen. User types project name: "Johnson Residence — Toowoomba".
Drags a PDF plan file onto the upload zone. Progress bar. Plan appears on the canvas.

**Voiceover:**
> "With Metricore, you upload your PDF plan — and you're measuring in seconds."

**Caption:**
`Upload any PDF plan. Start measuring in seconds.`

---

### [0:14 – 0:24] — SCALE CALIBRATION

**Screen:**
Canvas is live. User selects the Scale tool from the toolbar.
Draws a line along a wall with a printed dimension label (e.g., 8400mm).
A small dialog appears. User types: `8.4` and selects `metres`.
Clicks confirm. A green badge appears top-right: `✓ Scale 1:100`.

**Voiceover:**
> "Set the scale once by drawing over any known dimension on the plan. Metricore calibrates everything from there."

**Caption:**
`Set scale once. All measurements calibrate automatically.`

---

### [0:24 – 0:44] — MEASUREMENTS (the hero moment)

**Screen — sequence of 4 quick measurements:**

1. **Area tool** — User draws a polygon around the living room. It fills with a cyan tint. Label popup appears: user types "Living Room", selects trade "Flooring". Value appears in right panel: `42.3 m²`.

2. **Wall tool** — User clicks along the exterior walls. A wall line traces the perimeter. Label: "External Walls", trade "Brickwork". Value: `38.6 m`.

3. **Area tool again** — Roof outline drawn. "Roof", trade "Roofing". Value: `94.1 m²`.

4. **Count tool** — User clicks on each door symbol. Count appears: `6 items`. Trade: "Carpentry — Doors".

Right panel fills in real time as each measurement completes. Trade categories, quantities, and AUD values populate automatically.

**Voiceover:**
> "Draw your measurements directly on the plan — areas, walls, lengths, and counts. Every measurement is automatically sorted by trade and priced using current Australian rates."

**Caption:**
`Area. Wall. Length. Count. Priced instantly by trade.`

---

### [0:44 – 0:56] — ESTIMATE PANEL + TOTAL

**Screen:**
Camera slowly pans to the right-side estimate panel. It shows:
- Flooring: 42.3 m² — $3,890
- Brickwork: 38.6 m — $8,240
- Roofing: 94.1 m² — $14,620
- Carpentry — Doors: 6 items — $2,940
- **Total: $29,690**
- A small badge: `NCC references included`

User clicks the margin field, types `35`. Total updates to show sell price.

**Voiceover:**
> "Your itemised estimate builds in real time — materials, labour, and margin — all referenced against NCC requirements. Adjust your margin and the numbers update instantly."

**Caption:**
`Live estimate. NCC referenced. Adjust margin in real time.`

---

### [0:56 – 1:07] — EXPORT / TENDER

**Screen:**
User clicks "Generate Tender" button. A modal appears with two options: PDF and Excel.
User selects PDF. A professional tender document renders — Metricore-branded header, project name, itemised BOQ table, total, terms.
User clicks "Send to Client".

**Voiceover:**
> "When you're ready, generate a professional tender document in one click — PDF or Excel — ready to send to your client."

**Caption:**
`Professional tender. One click. PDF or Excel.`

---

Metricore logo centred on dark navy background.

metricore.com.au

**Voiceover:**
> "Metricore. From plans to tender — in minutes. Start your free trial at metricore.com.au."

**Caption:**
`metricore.com.au — 14-day free trial, no credit card.`

## Recording Checklist

- [ ] Use a real residential PDF plan (3–4 rooms, A3 or A1 scale)
- [ ] Pre-fill project name before recording — no typos on camera
- [ ] Set browser zoom to 100%, hide browser bookmarks bar
- [ ] Close all notifications (Do Not Disturb on)
- [ ] Use the largest cursor size available in System Preferences
- [ ] Record at 1440p minimum
- [ ] Do a dry run of the full flow before recording
- [ ] Record voiceover in a quiet room — not with the screen recording, add in post
- [ ] Add captions before publishing — 85% of social video is watched muted
